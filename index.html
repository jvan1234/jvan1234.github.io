About Me

Hello! My true name is Deregh Onureth Iskirthos. It is more common, however, for me to be called Wiz Superstar.

I do my best to help the village beneath my mountainous lair, but, well... My size does not help me too much.

My specially crafted size shrinking bracelets let me do things that I normally never could do!... However, it is unfortunate that some rogues think they can sneakily grab them; especially hilarious when they are running from my now true form.

I have been a furry for the past two years now, and it has been an amazing journey! I made lots of friends, received a lot of art, and even got a ton of opportunities! Since I'm still working on my degree in Cybersecurity, I'll gladly participate within the fandom through arts and programming!
